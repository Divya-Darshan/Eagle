# 🦅 **Eagle – A Fast & Fun Flappy Bird Style Game**

Welcome to **Eagle**, a lightweight and fast-paced **Flappy Bird–inspired game AKA Parody** built using **Godot 4.3**.  
Tap, flap, dodge, survive  and compete to get the highest score!

## 🎯 **About the Game**

**Eagle** is a simple yet addictive tap-to-fly arcade game.  
You control a tiny eagle trying to pass through endless pipes while the game becomes **faster and harder** over time.

Perfect for:
- quick play sessions  
- testing reflexes  
- beating your own high score  
- challenging your friends  

---

## 🕹️ **How to Play**

### **Mobile**
- Tap anywhere → flap upward  
- Avoid pipes  
- Survive as long as you can

### **PC**
- Press **Space** or **Enter** to flap  
- Same rules — don’t crash!
