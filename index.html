Eagle

Privacy Policy · February 2026

Eagle is a simple Flappy Bird–style parody game built with the Godot Engine. The game is designed to collect as little information as possible.

Information We Collect

Eagle does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, or location.

  • Game score
  • Temporary player identifier used only for leaderboard ranking

This information is used only to display scores on the online leaderboard.

Online Leaderboard

If you submit a score, it is sent to the Eagle leaderboard server to:

  • Display rankings
  • Compare scores between players

No personal identity information is required or stored.

Third-Party Services

The leaderboard service is hosted externally. These services may temporarily process gameplay data necessary to display scores.

Data Storage

Leaderboard data is stored only for gameplay and ranking purposes and may be removed or reset at any time.

Children's Privacy

Eagle does not knowingly collect personal data from children. The game is safe to play for all ages.

Changes To This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated in the future. Updates will be posted on this page.
