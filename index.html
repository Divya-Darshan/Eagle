Eagle Tap

Privacy Policy • Last Updated: February 2026

Eagle is a simple Flappy Bird style game built with the Godot Engine. We value your privacy and collect only the minimum information required to provide the online leaderboard.

Information We Collect

Eagle does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera data.

  • Your game score.
  • A temporary player identifier used only for leaderboard rankings.

How We Use Your Data

The limited information collected is used only to:

  • Display your score on the online leaderboard.
  • Rank players based on their scores.
  • Maintain normal gameplay features.

Online Leaderboard

When you submit a score, it is securely sent to the Eagle leaderboard server. No personal identity information is attached to your score.

Third-Party Services

The online leaderboard may use trusted third-party hosting providers. These services process only the gameplay information required to store and display leaderboard scores.

Data Storage

Leaderboard information is stored only for gameplay purposes. Scores may be reset or removed at any time without prior notice.

Children's Privacy

Eagle is suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any future updates will be published on this page.
